# iFLO Reflash #  
  
***Do At Your Own Risk ***  
  
I got tired of the iFLO miscounting my dispenses and leaving me with bottles still containing cleaner that I could not use because they have to be registered and are tracked. I also wanted in integrated into Home Assistant and off the cloud. The iFLO uses a ESP32-S2 SOLO. I mapped out most of what is needed.


![Pinout.png](https://github.com/demitryk1/SmartHome/blob/main/iFLO/Pinout.png?raw=true) 





### What You Need ###  
- philps head screwdriver   
- USB_Serial Converter, I used a SH-U09BL_CP2102N
- Something conductive to short the boot pin
